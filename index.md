I am a computer science engineer who graduated from [Mines Saint-Étienne](https://www.mines-stetienne.fr/) in France, specializing in formal methods and software security. Before that, I mainly studied mathematics in preparatory classes.

My interests include programming languages, static analysis, automated theorem proving, and the verification of software and hardware. I enjoy building program-analysis tools as much as studying the theory behind them.

I recently completed an internship with AWS's [Automated Reasoning Group](https://aws.amazon.com/what-is/automated-reasoning/) in Seattle, where I worked on stateful properties verification in [Strata](https://github.com/strata-org/Strata/). Previously, I developed the [Frama-C/Eva abstract debugger](https://www.frama-c.com/2026/02/02/Eva-abstract-debugger.html) at [CEA](https://www.cea.fr/), an interactive tool for understanding static-analysis results.

When I am away from the keyboard, I enjoy reading and climbing. Also, I am looking for a PhD program !
